Understand your estimate

Expected rate: the 10-year Treasury CMT plus the lender margin. This is a calculation assumption, not a quoted note rate.

Maximum claim amount: the lesser of the home value and the calculator's lending limit.

Principal limit: the maximum claim amount multiplied by the estimated principal limit factor. This calculator uses a PLF approximation, not an authoritative HUD table lookup.

Net proceeds: the estimated principal limit after mortgage payoff and estimated costs. Underwriting and other required set-asides may change the amount available.
